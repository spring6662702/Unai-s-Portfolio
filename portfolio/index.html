04 / AN EXAMPLE OF A WIN-WIN SITUATION IN MY LIFE

When I work on a school project with someone, we sometimes have different ideas about how to do it. Instead of insisting that my idea is the only right one, I try to find a solution that works for both of us. We share the tasks according to what each person is good at, listen to each other's suggestions and help each other when needed. This way, we both contribute, learn something and get a better result.

05 / EMPATHETIC PHRASES I HAVE RECEIVED OR SAID

Some phrases that make me feel understood and supported are: "I understand why you feel that way", "You have put a lot of effort into this", and "Don't worry, we can work it out together". I also try to say things like "I understand your point of view" and "Do you want to tell me what happened?". These words show that I am listening, that I respect the other person's feelings and that they can count on me.

06 / AN EXAMPLE OF SYNERGY IN MY LIFE

A good example of synergy is working as a team on a class assignment. Each person brings different skills and ideas: one may be better at researching, another at explaining, and another at organising or solving technical problems. By sharing our knowledge, communicating and combining our strengths, we can achieve a result that would be harder to reach individually. The final work becomes better because of everyone's contribution.
